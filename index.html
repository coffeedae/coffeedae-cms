Terms of service

Please read before ordering. By ordering, you agree to these terms.

Process & briefing

After you submit the form, I will chat with you about the brief and price through your main contact. If you have not heard from me within 2 days, please message me on any channel right away.

OK to draw

Fanart, OC, chibi, real people as chibi, middle-aged characters, omakase, gender-swapped characters, mimi (animal ears), light blood, OCxCANON (yume) all pairings! Normal scale suits female characters better than male; no very sharp handsome men, and my line style changes often.

Not drawn

NSFW, gore, robots / heavy armour, furry, elderly characters, immoral themes, very muscular bodies.

Payment

Kasikorn Bank / PromptPay / TrueMoney (no extra fee). 50% deposit or full payment before work starts. Your queue is confirmed once you send proof of payment.

Deliverables

Chibi: 2160×1620 px (130 dpi). Normal scale: up to the artist, but at least 2000×2000 px (130 dpi) for every image and scale.

Turnaround time

Chibi: 1-3 days. Normal scale: 1-14 days after entering the queue, depending on difficulty.

Commercial use & copyright

Prices are for personal use. Commercial use is charged ×2 on everything. Using the work to train AI or create NFTs is never allowed.

Revisions

2 free revisions at the sketch stage; each extra one is 10 THB. (Chibi Rough Omakase: no revisions, except for key details I forgot.) Once colouring starts, the pose and main structure cannot be changed.

Refund policy

If you cancel after the sketch has started, the deposit is not refunded. If I cancel, you get a 100% refund.

Portfolio use

I may post finished work on social media / my portfolio (tell me in advance if it must stay private or be posted on a set date). Any work may be used as a future commission sample; if you are not comfortable with that, tell me after confirming payment.

Delivery channels

Delivered via Google Drive or Gmail. Tell me in advance which you prefer; otherwise I will use Google Drive.

Credit

Please credit @coffeedae or the artist Facebook name whenever you post the work. Do not claim the art as your own.

Inquiry

If anything is unclear, message me through any contact channel before ordering.
